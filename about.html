Our story

Vishal Singh founded The Vault Dance in Indirapuram in 2018. The focus from day one: western styles like hip hop, jazz, ballet and contemporary, alongside Bollywood, taught step by step so complete beginners can keep up.

The Indirapuram studio, inside G.C. Bubbles School in Gyan Khand II, runs from early morning to late evening so school kids and working adults can both fit in a class. Away from the studio, the crew performs at societies, festivals and competitions.

In 2025 batches opened in Vaishali, and our newest studio now runs in Sector 16, Vasundhara. Students call it the TVD family, and some have been dancing with us for five years or more.
