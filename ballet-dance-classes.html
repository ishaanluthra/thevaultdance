What you will learn

Who it is for: Young kids starting out, and adults who want posture and grace.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Positions and posture

The five positions of the feet and arms, a lifted chest and a strong, straight back.

Barre work

Pliés, tendus and relevés that build ankle, knee and core strength.

Balance and turns

Holding positions on one leg, then simple turns with control.

Port de bras

Graceful arm movement that carries over into contemporary, jazz and semi-classical.

Inside a ballet class

  1. Barre

    Standing exercises holding the barre or a wall to warm up and build strength.

  2. Centre work

    The same exercises without support, focusing on balance.

  3. Across the floor

    Small jumps, chassés and turns travelling across the studio.

  4. Révérence

    A short bow and cool-down to finish.

Ballet questions

Anything else about ballet? Call 93115 04495.

What age can children start ballet?

Kids learn in their own batches. Tell us your child’s age and we will suggest the right one.

Do I need ballet shoes?

Socks are fine for the first classes. Your coach will tell you if and when to buy ballet shoes.

Is ballet only for girls?

No. Boys and men take ballet too, and it makes every other style cleaner.
