What you will learn

Who it is for: All ages. Most kids start here.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Hook steps

The signature moves from current and classic film songs.

Expressions

Using face, eyes and hands to sell the song, the heart of Bollywood dance.

Mixed styles

Bollywood borrows from hip hop, folk, semi-classical and jazz, so you pick up a bit of everything.

Stage-ready routines

Full songs choreographed for school functions, society events and sangeet nights.

Inside a bollywood class

  1. Warm-up

    A fun, high-energy warm-up to a film track.

  2. Song breakdown

    The song split into parts, with counts and lyrics as cues.

  3. Expressions and formations

    Adding faces, hands and group formations.

  4. Full song run

    Dancing the whole song together.

Can you choreograph a song for my function?

Yes. We choreograph for weddings, sangeet and school annual functions. See our wedding and events page.

Which songs do you teach?

A mix of trending and classic film songs, chosen to suit the batch’s age and level.

Is Bollywood good exercise?

Very. A Bollywood class is non-stop movement, so it is a fun cardio workout too.
