Choosing a class

Still unsure? A trial class is the quickest way to find out.

Which class is best for a complete beginner?

Bollywood, hip hop and dance fitness are the easiest to enjoy from day one. Ballet is a calm, focused start for young kids.

Can I take more than one style?

Yes. Many students mix styles, for example ballet with contemporary, or hip hop with jazz.

What are the class timings?

Studios open from 6 am and run batches until 10 pm. Exact batch times differ by style and branch, so message us for the current schedule.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the style, the number of classes a week and the branch. Call or WhatsApp for current fees and any running offers.
