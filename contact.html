Before you visit

Quick answers to what people ask first.

I have never danced before. Can I join?

Yes. Every style has beginner batches, and coaches break each move down step by step until it feels natural.

What are the class timings?

Studios open from 6 am and run batches until 10 pm. Exact batch times differ by style and branch, so message us for the current schedule.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the style, the number of classes a week and the branch. Call or WhatsApp for current fees and any running offers.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move and stretch in, and clean sports shoes. Bring a water bottle and a small towel.
