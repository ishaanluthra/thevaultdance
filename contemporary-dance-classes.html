Contemporary classes in Ghaziabad

What you will learn

Who it is for: Teens and adults, and younger dancers with some ballet or jazz.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Floor work

Rolling, sliding and moving in and out of the floor safely and smoothly.

Release and flow

Using breath, weight and momentum so movement looks effortless.

Spirals and contractions

Core-driven shapes that give contemporary its look.

Expression

Telling a story through a piece, so the audience feels it and not just sees it.

Inside a contemporary class

  1. Breath and warm-up

    Slow stretches and spine work to open up the body.

  2. Technique

    Floor work and release exercises.

  3. Choreography

    A phrase set to music, learnt in parts.

  4. Improvisation

    Short tasks that help you find your own way of moving.

Contemporary questions

Anything else about contemporary? Call 93115 04495.

Who teaches contemporary?

Priyanka leads our contemporary classes. Students say she is passionate and patient and breaks every step down.

Is contemporary hard for beginners?

It is very beginner friendly. You start with simple floor work and build up.

What should I wear?

Fitted clothes that cover your knees for floor work. Most students dance barefoot.

Pairs well with

BalletPosture, balance and clean technique. JazzSharp lines, turns, kicks and leaps, performed with attitude. Hip HopBounce, grooves, footwork and the confidence to freestyle.
