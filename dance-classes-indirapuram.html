Indirapuram studio questions

Families come here from Gyan Khand I to IV, Niti Khand, Shakti Khand, Ahinsa Khand, Abhay Khand, Nyay Khand, Vaibhav Khand, Shipra Suncity, Vasundhara and Vaishali.

Where is The Vault Dance Indirapuram?

53 Plot No, Gyan Khand II, Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201014. Inside G.C. Bubbles School. Look for the red arched gate.

What are the timings at Indirapuram?

Open every day, 6 am to 10 pm. Batch times change through the year, so WhatsApp us for the current schedule.

What ages do you teach?

Kids and adults, in separate batches. Tell us your child’s age when you call or WhatsApp and we will suggest the right batch.

I have never danced before. Can I join?

Yes. Every style has beginner batches, and coaches break each move down step by step until it feels natural.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the style, the number of classes a week and the branch. Call or WhatsApp for current fees and any running offers.
