Vasundhara studio questions

Families come here from Sectors 1 to 18, Vasundhara, Indirapuram, Vaishali, Prahlad Garhi, Mohan Nagar and Kaushambi.

Where is The Vault Dance Vasundhara?

Pocket B, Sector 16, Vasundhara, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201012. Near Maharaja Agrasen Marg, Sector 16.

What are the timings at Vasundhara?

Most days 6 am to 10 pm. Friday mornings only, Sunday from 9 am. Batch times change through the year, so WhatsApp us for the current schedule.

What ages do you teach?

Kids and adults, in separate batches. Tell us your child’s age when you call or WhatsApp and we will suggest the right batch.

I have never danced before. Can I join?

Yes. Every style has beginner batches, and coaches break each move down step by step until it feels natural.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the style, the number of classes a week and the branch. Call or WhatsApp for current fees and any running offers.
