What you will learn

Who it is for: Adults of every fitness level.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Cardio dance

Simple, repeatable dance moves to upbeat music that keep your heart rate up.

Strength and toning

Bodyweight moves worked into routines for legs, core and arms.

Yoga

Asanas, breathing and stretching for strength, balance and flexibility.

Consistency

Morning and evening batches so a workout fits around work.

Inside a dance fitness and yoga class

  1. Warm-up

    Gentle moves to raise the heart rate.

  2. Main workout

    Dance blocks to a mix of Bollywood and pop tracks, or a yoga sequence.

  3. Core and strength

    Short focused sets.

  4. Stretch and breathe

    Cool-down and relaxation.
