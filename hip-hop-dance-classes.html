Hip Hop classes in Ghaziabad

Bounce, grooves, footwork and the confidence to freestyle. Hip hop is where most Vault students start, and where many of our stage pieces come from.

What you will learn

Who it is for: Kids, teens and adults. No experience needed.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Grooves and bounce

The rocks, bounces and body grooves that every hip hop move sits on. You learn to stay relaxed and in the pocket of the beat.

Footwork and transitions

Steps that travel, turn and change direction cleanly, so combinations flow instead of stopping and starting.

Musicality

Hitting accents, riding the bass line and switching between half time and double time without losing the count.

Choreography and freestyle

Full routines broken into eight-counts, then space to freestyle so the moves become yours.

Inside a hip hop class

  1. Warm up to the beat

    Joint rolls, stretches and simple grooves to get the body moving with the music.

  2. Foundation drills

    Two or three core moves repeated slowly, then at tempo.

  3. Choreography

    The routine taught in sections, with time to clean each one.

  4. Run it

    Full run-throughs in groups, with notes from the coach.

Hip Hop questions

Anything else about hip hop? Call 93115 04495.

Is hip hop good for kids?

Yes. It builds coordination, rhythm and confidence, and kids enjoy the music. Kids and adults learn in separate batches.

What shoes should I wear?

Clean, flat sports shoes with some grip. Avoid running shoes with thick heels.

Do you teach jazz funk too?

Yes. Jazz funk mixes hip hop grooves with jazz lines and is taught alongside our hip hop and jazz batches.

Pairs well with

JazzSharp lines, turns, kicks and leaps, performed with attitude. BollywoodFilm songs, hook steps and big expressions. ContemporaryFloor work, release and moving with feeling instead of counting.
