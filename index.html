Questions parents ask us

Anything else? Call 93115 04495 and we will answer it.

What ages do you teach?

Kids and adults, in separate batches. Tell us your child’s age when you call or WhatsApp and we will suggest the right batch.

I have never danced before. Can I join?

Yes. Every style has beginner batches, and coaches break each move down step by step until it feels natural.

What are the class timings?

Studios open from 6 am and run batches until 10 pm. Exact batch times differ by style and branch, so message us for the current schedule.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the style, the number of classes a week and the branch. Call or WhatsApp for current fees and any running offers.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move and stretch in, and clean sports shoes. Bring a water bottle and a small towel.

Do students perform on stage?

Yes. Our dancers perform at stage shows, competitions, society celebrations and festivals, and students who want to perform are prepared for it in class.

Do you choreograph for weddings and school functions?

Yes. We choreograph sangeet and wedding performances, school annual functions and society events.
