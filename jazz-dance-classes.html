What you will learn

Who it is for: Kids and adults who want clean technique with plenty of energy.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Turns

Spotting, balance and clean single then double pirouettes.

Kicks and leaps

Flexibility and strength for high kicks, jetés and travelling jumps.

Lines and isolations

Long arms, pointed feet and isolations of the head, chest and hips.

Jazz funk

Commercial choreography that blends jazz with hip hop grooves, the style you see in music videos.

Inside a jazz class

  1. Warm up and stretch

    Cardio warm-up followed by stretching for hips, hamstrings and back.

  2. Across the floor

    Turns, kicks and leaps travelling from one side of the studio to the other.

  3. Choreography

    A short routine built over the week.

  4. Performance run

    Dancing it full out, with facial expression and attack.

Jazz questions

Anything else about jazz? Call 93115 04495.

Do I need to be flexible to start?

No. Flexibility improves with regular stretching in class. Everyone starts where they are.

Is jazz different from hip hop?

Jazz focuses on lines, turns and kicks. Hip hop is more grounded and groove based. Many students take both.

What should I wear?

Fitted clothes so the coach can see your lines, and jazz shoes or clean socks.

Pairs well with

BalletPosture, balance and clean technique. Hip HopBounce, grooves, footwork and the confidence to freestyle. ContemporaryFloor work, release and moving with feeling instead of counting.
