From parents and students

Questions parents ask

Something else on your mind? Call and talk to a coach.

What age can my child start?

Kids learn in their own batches by age. Tell us your child’s age and we will suggest the right batch and style.

My child is shy. Will they cope?

Most shy kids open up within a few classes once they are dancing in a group of kids their age. Let the coach know and they will ease your child in.

Will my child get to perform?

Kids perform at annual functions, society events and our shows when they feel ready.

When are kids batches?

Afternoon and evening batches fit around school. Message us for current timings at your studio.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable clothes, clean sports shoes or socks, and a water bottle.
