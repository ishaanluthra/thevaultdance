What you will learn

Who it is for: Kids and adults who love graceful, expressive dance.

Batches run at Indirapuram, Vaishali and Vasundhara, with beginners and experienced dancers taught separately.

Footwork

Rhythmic, Kathak-inspired footwork and spins.

Mudras

Hand gestures that carry meaning in Indian dance.

Abhinaya

Facial expression and storytelling to bring lyrics to life.

Grace and posture

Soft, controlled movement with a strong, upright frame.

Inside a semi-classical class

  1. Namaskar and warm-up

    A short salutation, then stretches for feet, knees and back.

  2. Footwork

    Rhythm patterns practised slowly, then at speed.

  3. Choreography

    A film or fusion song broken into parts.

  4. Expression work

    Adding mudras and abhinaya to the routine.

Semi-Classical questions

Do you teach Kathak?

Kathak and other classical-inspired styles are listed with us. Ask about current batches at your nearest studio.

Is semi-classical good for functions?

Yes. It is a favourite for festivals, school events and wedding performances.

What should I wear?

A comfortable kurta or salwar suit, or fitted clothes you can move in. Most dance barefoot.

Pairs well with

BollywoodFilm songs, hook steps and big expressions. BalletPosture, balance and clean technique. ContemporaryFloor work, release and moving with feeling instead of counting.
