What we choreograph

From one couple to a whole family, or a full school class.

Sangeet and family dances

Medleys for cousins, parents and grandparents, with simple steps everyone can manage.

Couple and bridal entries

A first dance or entry that looks polished without months of practice.

School annual functions

Group pieces for classes and houses, choreographed to suit the age and stage.

Society and festival events

Our dancers perform at society celebrations and festival pujas, and we choreograph for residents too.

How it works

  1. Tell us the plan

    Date, venue, number of people and the songs you have in mind.

  2. Pick the songs

    We help you choose songs and the order that works on stage.

  3. Rehearse

    Sessions at your nearest studio, or at home on request, building the routine section by section.

  4. Final run

    A full dress rehearsal so everyone walks on stage confident.

Ask about a performance

Event choreography questions

Send us your date and songs and we will reply with a plan.

How many rehearsals will we need?

It depends on the number of songs, the group size and how much dance experience everyone has. Tell us your plan and we will suggest a schedule.

None of us can dance. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Choreography is built around your group, with simple steps for beginners and a few highlights for the confident ones.

Can you rehearse at our home?

Rehearsals usually happen at our studios. Home sessions can be arranged on request.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on songs, people and rehearsals. Message us your details for a quote.

How early should we book?

As early as you can. Wedding season fills up fast, and more time means more relaxed rehearsals.
