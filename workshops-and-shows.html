Workshops, shows and competitions

Classes are where you learn. Workshops, competitions and shows are where it clicks. Here is what happens beyond the weekly batch.

Get workshop updatesFollow on Instagram

Workshops

Short, focused sessions with guest artists and our own coaches. Open to Vault students and anyone who wants to try.

Guest artists

“Two artists. Two unique styles. One stage.” Workshops bring in choreographers with a different take on movement.

Simple pricing

Past single workshops have cost ₹500, and some have been free. Each one is announced with its price.

Every level

Choreography is taught from scratch, so beginners can keep up and experienced dancers get pushed.

Beyond Ghaziabad

Our first Noida workshop ran in March 2025.

On stage

Shows and tributes

Full productions, including a Michael Jackson “Smooth Criminal” tribute.

Competitions

Vault crews compete, and in 2025 brought home a winning performance.

Society and festival events

Performances at society pujas, festivals and community events.

Student showcases

Students who want to perform are prepared for it in class and dance alongside the crew.
